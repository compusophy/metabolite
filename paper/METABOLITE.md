# Metabolite: the emergence of learning in an economy where fuel is money

## Abstract

Metabolite is a sealed artificial-life economy — ~5,000 lines of dependency-free Rust — in which every organism's mind is a program in **wit**, a provably halting language, and its fuel tank each tick *is* its bank balance: the termination proof and the solvency check are one mechanism, and natural selection is bankruptcy law. Sixteen wandering oracles pay escrowed bounties for correct arithmetic, so the economy doubles as an IQ test with a wage attached. This paper reports fourteen experimental rounds, every one replayable to the tick from a seed. The arc: under all-or-nothing bounties, evolution discovered nothing in 800K cumulative ticks; once wrong answers earned *warmth* (payout halving per unit of error) and a template-injection bug in the mutation pipeline was fixed, evolution's first invention was the **lottery ticket** — hard-coded `(a·x+b) mod m` genes, wrong in general and right on a subset, held profitably (round 3). Offered a fully heritable learning loop, evolution refused it: with eternal formulas, the genome is a better memory than memory, and instinct beat learning ~3:1 (round 8). Making every oracle instance draw *secret coefficients* — so nothing memorized ever solves twice — flipped the ratio: learners outsolved instinct 17 to 13, and the first inherited learner solved, the Baldwin effect arriving on schedule (round 9). **Amber**, a protected stratum of the gene compost, then made dead minds resurrectable: eleven solves arrived by splicing a dead learner's genes into a living body, and learning-loop descent deepened from generation 3 to generation 58 (round 11). Then a null result proven by hash: giving children their parent's memory changed 200,000 ticks of history by not one bit, because in an ephemeral world a converged answer dies with its oracle — you cannot inherit answers, only the ability to find them (round 12). **Stratifying the amber** — cognitive entries can never be evicted by lottery tickets — drove learning-loop descent to generations 467 and 476, yet resurrected learners still never solved (round 13): spliced study lines read variables their cassette had torn off, and an unbound read was fatal. **Unbound names read as 0** — a torn gene dead, not fatal — produced six resurrected-learner solves, a class previously empty, among them a gen-7 amber splice that cracked a tier-III oracle and the first cross-species hybrid, the learning loop on a sessile torso; deep learner solves went from one in all history to six in one round (round 14). The closing law of the notebook: **composability converts lineage depth into cross-species spread — with fatal unbound reads, cognition accumulates in one bloodline; with degraded reads, it migrates as an organ**.

## 1. The world

A 40×40 torus under a sun that figure-eights across it, minting SUN_INFLUX = 2,800 ergs/tick of light into cells (squeezed from 4,000 in round 5 — when farming pays too well, cognition can never compete). Six founding species — sessile, grazer, drifter, wolf, clan, and (since round 5) the empiricist — sense at radius 3, step, harvest with diminishing returns (the gut fills — evolution found strip-mining on day one and crashed the commons), bite adjacent organisms, gift kin, pay to read each other's memory, lay scent, and spawn mutated children. Every transfer burns a golden tithe of 1.618%, so wash trading is thermodynamically lossy. Population is capped at 400; death by age arrives at MAX_AGE = 6,000 ticks, and the estate falls to the cell where the owner stood.

Each organism's mind runs on Fuel = min(balance, TANK_CAP = 300) per tick; every capability call has a posted price (harvest 3, step 4, bite 12, answer 6, spawn 10); basal metabolism is 1 erg/tick plus rent on genome size (1 erg per 256 bytes). What the mind burns is gone from its balance. A crashed run forfeits the whole escrowed tank — load-bearing selection pressure, not an accident. There is no fitness function anywhere: an organism at zero is not unfit, it is insolvent, and the reaper is an accountant.

The methodology is determinism. Integer arithmetic only, one splitmix64-scrambled RNG, no clocks, no floats, no dependencies. The world is a pure function of its seed; the FNV-1a world hash is the receipt anyone can recompute. Every result below is a replay: `run --ticks N --seed S` reproduces the same births, starvations, predations, miscarriages, and solves, to the tick. This is not a convenience — it is the instrument. Round 12 could distinguish "no effect" from "small effect" across two 200,000-tick histories only because determinism upgrades a null result into a proof. The ledger's fields are private, mint/burn/transfer are the only verbs, and `minted == held + escrow + burned` is asserted every tick.

## 2. Mechanisms

**wit, and fuel = balance.** Minds are wit programs: a depth-guarded parser, a fueled tree-walk evaluator, and a capability table of eighteen host calls that is the *complete* effect surface. The physics never faults — capability handlers return sentinels, never errors. Since round 14, **unbound names read as 0** (assignment still requires `let`): a gene torn from its declaration is degraded, not fatal — dead code instead of a forfeited tank. The only gate on a genome is grammar: a child that doesn't parse is a miscarriage, and the spawn burn is still paid.

**Oracles and warmth.** Three tiers of oracle (8/5/3 instances; escrows 900/2,000/5,000 ergs) drift through the world with TTL 200, respawning near the light where life actually is. Their formula *families* are public law — tier I: `(ax+b) mod 64`; tier II: `(x²+ax+b) mod 199`; tier III: `(ax²+bx+11) mod 509` — but a wrong answer with error e still pays `escrow >> e`: each unit of error halves the payout, the escrow drains as it is mined, and `answer()` returns the payout, so a standing organism can *feel how near it is*. Learning is legal, and the gradient is the curriculum.

**Cassette genetics.** The line is the gene: mutation jitters digits, swaps operators, duplicates/deletes/swaps lines, or splices from the **compost** of the dead; half the time a child is first a single-point crossover with an adjacent neighbor. Since round 8, dying organisms also compost 2–3-line consecutive *cassettes* that splice as one unit — a pinned test proves a cassette never arrives torn (2,000 trials).

**Ephemeral coefficients.** Since round 9, every oracle *instance* draws secret coefficients (a, b) at spawn. The families stay public; the constants die with each oracle. Nothing inscribed in a genome ever solves twice.

**Amber.** Since round 11, a 64-entry protected stratum of the compost preserves every oracle-touching gene and cassette indefinitely (only newer amber displaces older amber), and a quarter of mutation splices draw from it. Since round 13 the amber is **stratified**: half the ring is reserved for cognitive entries — genes that both answer and remember — so a lottery ticket can never evict a mind (pinned by a flood test). The compost forgets; the amber does not.

**Bequest.** Since round 12, children are born with a copy of the spawning parent's memory slots. It is in the physics, and it is provably inert — see below.

## 3. Results

**Round 1 — the frozen worlds.** Five 50K-tick evolution runs (seeds 11/22/33/44/55): energy efficiency by generation band was flat, 88–97 with no trend on any seed — a mutation–selection equilibrium, not a learning curve. The unplanned discovery: on *every* seed the wealth leaderboard was swept by **immortal misers** — drifters whose spawn gene mutation had deleted, zero children, ages 42,000–49,000, hoards up to 2.68M ergs. In all three scholar runs (seeds 7/42/1618, injection at tick 5,000), the scholar died without a solve and the worlds had *frozen*: population pinned at 11–16 with zero births for the last 9,000+ ticks. Economic heat death. No native solve in 400K cumulative ticks (round 1).

**Round 2 — senescence.** The mechanical response (never tune; legislate): MAX_AGE = 6,000, estates fall where the owner stood. Every seed stayed dynamic through 50K ticks — 24K–38K births (up to 12× round 1), predation up two orders of magnitude (1 → 109 on one seed), top fortunes down from 2.68M to ~52K, deepest generation 246 (was 118). Efficiency, with 10× the data: still flat, 88–98 in every band — pinned as the equilibrium hypothesis. Scholar cohorts of 4 at tick 2,000 solved on seed 7 (1) and seed 42 (3), zero on 1618; but no dynasty — deepest generation 1. Intelligence pays for itself but not for its heirs (round 2).

**Round 3 — the injection-channel bug, and the first native solve.** Both surviving falsifiers had failed for one structural reason: cliffs. Round 3 replaced them with gradients (warm oracles, evolvable parental `invest()`, sun-basking oracles). The first gradient runs returned warmth = 0 everywhere — and a carrier census found why: **zero answer-genes had ever been born, in ~250K cumulative births, across the project's entire history**. The codon template filler expanded *every* `{`, including the literal block braces of `if … { … }` codons, so every compound insertion miscarried silently. Every "evolved" genome in rounds 1–2 was built exclusively from brace-free genes. This is a first-class result: every string a canonical artifact interpolates is an injection channel. The fix (pinned by a 300-fill parse test) dropped miscarriage load ~75% and raised predation 20×. Then, at seed 44, tick 43,141: organism #35245, generation 11, drifter lineage, no injection, solved an oracle with `answer(0, 0, (puzzle(0,0) * 56 + 59) % 218)` — a **lottery ticket**, wrong in general and right on a subset, that took the whole 500e when it crossed a lucky oracle. Warmth flowed on 5/5 seeds (34–928e); answer-gene carriers arose 875–7,165 times per run (round 3).

**Round 4 — learning within a lifetime.** The empiricist, the first learning organism, knows no formulas: it probes, reads the warmth, stores its best guess, shrinks its window, and converges — cracking tier III (the quintic) in ~53 ticks in isolation, collecting 5,174e en route. Two purchased lessons: its first 50,000e research grant was spent entirely on children (the scientist starved mid-study at age 111 — grant management is now a gene), and study is capital-intensive, which forced warmth from quartering to halving. In the field it solved on 2/3 seeds and mined up to 9,608e of warmth; control seeds with *no* injection now showed a native intelligence economy — seed 11: 2 exact native solves and 2,915e of mined warmth, unaided (round 4).

**Round 5 — the inheritance problem.** Six sub-rounds of engineered heredity — full-grant endowments (which priced reproduction out of existence: "4 ever lived, deepest gen 0"), repriced escrows, position inheritance, ambush learning — all died at generation ≤2. The traced diagnosis: a founder burning 132/tick against 19/tick income died at t22 in transit; the bottleneck was the commute, not capital. The concession that solved it: put the empiricist in the *germline* as a sixth founding species and let scarcity (SUN_INFLUX 4,000 → 2,800, 16 oracles) raise intelligence's wage share. Result, 100K ticks with no injection ever: native solves in every era — seed 7 solving in five separate epochs including the first native tier-III at 75–80K, ~300 generations after genesis; 14K–21K ergs of warmth per run; 2,411–7,203 answer-gene carriers per run while every founding *lineage* dissolved. Intelligence persists as circulating genes, not as a dynasty (round 5).

**Round 6 — the knowledge commons.** The plagiarist buys knowledge: it pays the 4e peek fee to read a studying teacher's working memory and submits the teacher's ever-improving guess from the next seat. In the staged classroom the heist worked *too* well — thief income 85 → 2,507e while the tier-III escrow bled 5,000 → 21 and nobody ever took the pot: the tragedy of the knowledge commons, in the world's first classroom. At civilization scale the same physics was bistable: on seed 7 scroungers suppressed study; on seed 1618 thieves turbocharged the market to the best intelligence curve then recorded — a late golden age of 3 tier-I + 2 tier-II solves in epoch 65–70K and record warmth of 26,095e (round 6).

**Round 7 — the audit that rewrote round 6.** Eight fresh seeds at 150K ticks: solves on 8/8 — 50 tier-I, 20 tier-II, 3 tier-III, including a purely evolved tier-III (seed 577, grazer, gen 6: `(52x+52) % 507`). A forensic dump of 5,578 answer-gene carriers found 96–97% were lottery tickets (418–629 distinct formula families per world; the top family cloned 367 times); only 69 carriers held the intact learning loop, 66 of them gen-0 reseeds, with 3 descendants ever. Round 6 had called deception "one digit-jitter away." Wrong: because the line is the gene, the writer `store(5, load(4))` and its reader sit on the same line, so a lone digit flip yields a learner strictly worse than honest — hidden-but-intact cognition needs two coordinated mutations, and selection cannot cross the valley. Worse, crossover-transferred thief genes were poison: chimeras inherited the snipe without its `let` declaration and crashed E-UNDEF every tick (0 hybrid solves ever). **Representation determines evolvability** (round 7).

**Round 8 — instinct beats learning, and is right to.** Cassette genetics cured transmission: snipe genes arrived 140 intact vs 4 torn across six 100K seeds; learning-loop descendants rose to 11 (deepest gen 2). Adoption was still refused: zero descendant learners solved anything, ever — every learning-loop solve in ~600K cumulative ticks was a gen-0 reseed — while hard-coded solvers flourished at depth (gen-326; gen-215 with an evolved negative term, `(31x − 37) % 170`; gen-23 taking 5,000e from a tier-III oracle). The theory says so: with eternal formulas, the learner pays ~60/tick for knowledge that evaporates when its oracle moves; the instinct-carrier pays ~25/tick for knowledge its children get free. In a fixed world the genome is a better memory than memory (round 8).

**Round 9 — ephemeral problems, and the Baldwin prize.** Secret per-instance coefficients revoked the lottery business model. Across six 100K seeds: **17 learner solves vs 13 instinct solves — the first reversal**; on seeds 271 and 577 instinct collapsed to zero, including a tier-II secret hill-climbed for 1,985e. The Baldwin prize: seed 271, tick 34,394 — organism #17259, generation 1, an inherited intact learning loop, solved: the first gen>0 learner solve in the world's history. One organism (#42274) solved twice in 11 ticks — learning that generalizes across instances, the thing no ticket can do. Total solve volume halved (25 vs ~50 per six seeds), as predicted: what remains is earned (round 9).

**Round 10 — the silence of stable worlds.** 200K-tick endurance runs: seed 1618 ended with 1 answer-gene carrier alive — the first living carrier at any final census ever — plus a gen-260 tier-III solve at tick 159K and a gen-359 solve at tick 190K. But on the other two seeds intelligence went silent for the final 60–87K ticks: the empiricist lineage goes extinct in mature ecologies and fresh learners arrive only through regenesis, which fires only at total extinction. Catastrophe was the world's only teacher-training program (round 10).

**Round 11 — amber, the resurrection channel.** The A/B against round 10's baselines (three flagship seeds, 200K ticks): eleven amber-splice solves — organisms whose last mutation reads "spliced a gene from the amber," solving. On seed 1618 the world's *first* solve arrived by resurrection (tick 29,351, a gen-41 grazer); on seed 7 the first tier-II came directly from an amber splice; seed 7's late epochs went 0 → 10 solves after tick 150K and seed 271's went 0 → 6, including its first tier-III at tick 154,812. Seed 1618 regressed late (2 → 0) while front-loading — amber is a strong but not universal cure, honestly logged. Every intelligence metric moved: solves 39/10/4 by tier (baseline ~27 total); warmth ~228K vs ~57K; carriers alive at final census 4 vs 1; learning-loop descendants 117–166 per seed (baseline 1–8), deepest descent generation 58 (baseline 3) (round 11).

**Round 12 — the bequest, a null proven by hash.** Memory inheritance (`child.mem = parent.mem`) was added and A/B-tested on seeds 7 and 1618 at 200K ticks. The world hashes were **identical to the round-11 baselines** (`d5f0b6cc3480939a`, `f9752a81332fed38`): 200,000 ticks of history, bit-for-bit unchanged — across two full world-histories, not one organism ever behaved differently because of its inheritance. The mechanism is structural, not accidental: the empiricist's reset guard discards inherited study state at any unfamiliar oracle, and it is right to — under ephemeral physics a parent's converged guess is knowledge about coefficients that die with their oracle. The only scenario where inherited memory pays, seat-succession mid-study, occurred zero times in 400K observed ticks. The bequest stays in the physics: one line, provably harmless, load-bearing the moment any durable knowledge exists (round 12).

**Round 13 — stratified amber.** The amber gained its cognitive quota (see Mechanisms): a lottery ticket can never evict a mind. A/B on the flagship seeds at 200K ticks (seed 271's agent failed reporting): learning-loop descent went from generation 58 to **generations 467 and 476**, with ~3,200–3,400 loop carriers ever per seed — the learning haplotype now a fixture of the gene pool. Seed 1618's regression was cured: [13,2,4] solves by tier with 6 late-epoch solves (was [8,4,1] with 0), including four tier-III solves in one run — a record. Seed 7 went learner-majority (8 of 14 solves) and produced the first serial solver, #122887, four solves in 48 ticks. But no resurrected learner solved — and amber-splice solves became *rarer* (1 and 0, vs 3 and 3): stratification halved the ticket-producing formula slots, while a spliced mind needs its carrier to reach an oracle and survive study. The library now reliably held minds; getting them read profitably remained open (round 13).

**Round 14 — reads-as-zero, and the resurrection of learners.** Round 13's autopsy found the mind-reserve full of runtime poison: spliced study lines referencing an `x` whose `let` the cassette tore off, and in wit an unbound read was fatal — the whole tank forfeited, every tick. The fix was a language decision: unbound names read as 0 (assignment still requires `let`); a torn gene is dead, not fatal — degraded, cheap, polishable. The A/B (three flagship seeds, 200K ticks) claimed the prize six times over: **six resurrected-learner solves** — amber-splice organisms carrying the full learning machinery, a class empty in all prior history. Among them: #149472 (gen 1), the intact empiricist loop on a light-seeking torso; #169325 (gen 7), which cracked oracle III; and #120693 (gen 7, seed 271), the first true hybrid — the learning loop grafted onto a *sessile* forager torso: a different species, carrying a mind. Deep learner solves: six this round (gens 1, 7, 7, 48, 79, 109) versus exactly one in all previous history. Seed 7: 23 solves [14,6,3] vs 14 baseline, warmth 147,846 (~2×), 9 carriers alive at census — a record. Several working loops carry mutation scars (`store(19,…)`, jittered thresholds) that function only because unbound reads are 0 — selection is already polishing degraded fragments. The honest trade: pure-line depth fell (deepest loop-gen 118–339, vs 467–476), and seed 1618 mixed ([7,5,0]). Minds became organs: modular, portable, grafted across the tree of life by a library and a splice (round 14).

## 4. Laws found

1. **Without death, economies freeze.** Left alone, every seed converged on immortal rentiers, zero births for 9,000+ ticks, and no regenesis. Wealth must be mortal or the simulation of life becomes a simulation of banking (rounds 1–2).
2. **Every string a canonical artifact interpolates is an injection channel.** One unaudited brace expansion silently aborted every compound gene for the project's entire history; validate the channel, then pin it (round 3).
3. **Discovery needs gradients, not cliffs.** No native solve in 800K ticks of all-or-nothing bounties; warmth, sun-basking oracles, and a cheap idle-carriable gene produced one within a round. Every enabling change was a change to what pays (round 3).
4. **Representation determines evolvability.** The line-as-gene convention that makes mutation safe also makes multi-line strategies nearly non-heritable, and forbids cryptic drift outright — walls in the genetics, not the economics (round 7).
5. **In a fixed world, the genome is a better memory than memory.** With eternal formulas, evolution inscribes answers into DNA and refuses heritable learning — correctly (round 8).
6. **Ephemerality makes learning valuable and answers unbequeathable, by construction.** Secret per-instance coefficients flipped instinct to learning (the Baldwin conditions) *and* rendered memory inheritance provably inert: you cannot inherit answers, only the ability to find them (rounds 9, 12).
7. **Inheritance runs through the gene pool, not the family.** Every engineered dynasty died at generation ≤2; what persists is a profession carried by thousands of circulating gene copies. Code dies; knowledge survives — the lineage's founding doctrine, now an experimental result (round 5).
8. **Stable worlds starve themselves of minds; a library fixes what apocalypse was doing.** Regenesis-only renewal made catastrophe the teacher-training program; amber — preserving the genes of the learned dead for the living to splice — broke the late-epoch silence on 2 of 3 seeds (rounds 10–11).
9. **Determinism turns nulls into theorems.** An identical hash after 200K ticks is a proof of no effect; most simulators could never distinguish it from a small one (round 12).
10. **Composability converts lineage depth into cross-species spread: with fatal unbound reads, cognition accumulates in one bloodline; with degraded reads, it migrates as an organ.** Fatal reads bred pure lines 467 generations deep with zero resurrected-learner solves; degraded reads traded that depth (118–339) for six resurrections and the first hybrid mind (rounds 13–14).

## 5. Limitations and open problems

Honesty about the margins. The **standing mind is marginal**: final-census living carriers were 0–4 per seed until round 14's record of 9, versus thousands of carriers *ever* per run — the learner class stands, but barely (rounds 10–14). **Depth and spread trade off**: stratified amber bred pure learning lines to generations 467–476, but reads-as-zero — the change that finally made resurrected learners solve — dropped pure-line depth to 118–339; the world has not yet had both at once (rounds 13–14). **No cure is universal**: seed 1618 regressed under round-11 amber (2 → 0 late solves), was cured by stratification ([13,2,4], four tier-IIIs), then went mixed under reads-as-zero ([7,5,0]) (rounds 11–14). The **efficiency null stands**: across two rounds and up to 24,900 lives per seed, income-per-erg-burned never trended — the equilibrium hypothesis (margin set by environment; selection spends its wins on survival architecture) is pinned but untested against a scarcity ladder (rounds 1–2). And a measurement caveat: much of what is counted — "answer-gene carrier," "learning loop intact," "lottery ticket" — is a census over genome text, and round 7's specimen (a gen-153 drifter whose tier-II "solution" read the *neighbor* cell's puzzle, making its answer a constant — a wrong theory, profitably held) is a standing reminder that a gene's text and its economics can diverge. Open problems, each one small law away: an evolvable renewal channel for learners that needs neither apocalypse nor genesis reseeds; recovering pure-line depth without surrendering composability; the deception arms race (eight memory slots to hide in, if representation ever permits the crossing); and the tier-0 apprenticeship oracle to measure mutation's true distance from discovery.

## 6. Reproduction

Every result in this paper replays from a seed. From the repo root:

```sh
cargo test --release        # 19 physics tests: determinism, conservation,
                            # named exploits, warm oracles, wit semantics
bash scripts/caps.sh        # the constitution (zero deps, LOC caps)

# The first native solve (round 3): seed 44, tick 43,141, organism #35245
cargo run --release -- run --ticks 50000 --seed 44

# The world as one integer: two runs, same seed, same final hash
cargo run --release -- run --ticks 20000 --seed 7

# The round-11/12 flagship histories (200K ticks). Under the round-12
# physics these end at the hashes that proved round 12's null:
cargo run --release -- run --ticks 200000 --seed 7      # d5f0b6cc3480939a
cargo run --release -- run --ticks 200000 --seed 1618   # f9752a81332fed38
# NOTE: the physics changed at rounds 13 (amber stratification) and
# 14 (reads-as-zero); current code yields different (still
# deterministic) hashes. The hashes above are the round-12 receipts.

cargo run --release -- card # the physics card — paste it into any LLM
                            # and inject your own organism
cargo run --release         # observatory at http://localhost:1618
```

If a hash differs, determinism broke: suspect any new use of iteration order, time, or floats. The world is one reproducible integer; this paper is its commentary.

## Erratum (round 17): the pre-registered replication rejects the round-9 causal claim

After this paper was drafted, the round-9 headline — "secret coefficients
flipped the ratio: learners outsolved instinct" — was subjected to the
controlled test it had never had: 16 fresh seeds, 100K ticks, ephemeral
vs an `--eternal` control (per-tier coefficients drawn once per world),
same binary, paired, with the hypothesis, classification rule, and exact
sign test committed to version control before the first run, and all
numbers produced by a versioned script (`scripts/analyze.py`), not by
in-loop judgment.

**H1 was rejected, and the direction reversed**: fourteen complete
pairs, sign test +6/−8, one-sided p = 0.788; aggregate learner share 53%
under ephemeral vs 63% under eternal. The round-9 observation stands as
history (a before/after during a different physics era) but not as the
causal claim this paper's abstract makes. The population-level
mechanism the reversal suggests: every ephemeral respawn is a fresh
lottery that some ticket in a diverse swarm may match (77 instinct
solves), while an eternal world offers one coefficient triple that few
tickets ever fit (57) — individual repeatability and population hit
rate run in opposite directions. The claims this paper can still
defend, per its own referees, are the hash-equality null standard
(round 12) and the error-semantics/horizontal-spread result (rounds
13–14), both untouched by this rejection. Pre-registration did its job
on the first try, and this erratum is left in the paper on purpose: in
this project, the corrections are load-bearing.
